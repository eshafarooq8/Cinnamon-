ABOUT US

In 2018, we planted the seeds of Cinnimon's culinary vision, and by 2023, our doors swung open, revealing a restaurant that goes beyond the ordinary. Cinnimon is more than a business; it's a dream nurtured through seasons, now a vibrant culinary haven. The name reflects our commitment—Cinnimon is not just a spice; it's the embodiment of all seasons, infusing warmth and flavor into every dish, with a touch of sweetness that elevates the dining experience. Stepping into Cinnimon, you enter a sanctuary where culinary artistry shines. Our chefs, like skilled artists, craft dishes that transcend mere sustenance. The menu is a curated adventure, exploring flavors inspired by the spirit of the seasons. Cinnimon invites you to indulge in the richness of our offerings, from cinnamon-infused delights to the lingering sweetness in every bite. Each dish tells a story, a journey that began with a vision and continues with every welcomed guest. Welcome to Cinnimon, where every season is an invitation to savor the artistry of flavor.
